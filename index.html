04About

More than
a Data Scientist.

My work sits at the intersection of data science, machine learning and financial services. I spent three years in regulated banking, working across financial data, credit risk and compliance, before moving into data science and AI.

I bring that context into the way I design AI systems, so technical decisions stay connected to real operational problems. I care about systems that can be evaluated, explained, monitored and trusted, not just models that perform well in a notebook.

Before data science, I was a Data and Compliance Analyst at First Bank of Nigeria, auditing client data and supporting KYC and enhanced due diligence, and then a Financial Data Analyst and Credit Risk Officer at Access Bank, analysing customer and transaction data to support credit decisions.

I went on to complete an MSc in Data Science in London. Today I focus on fraud detection, credit risk and agentic AI for regulated environments — treating explainability, governance and deployment as part of the build rather than an afterthought.

About

I came to data science through banking, not the other way around.

Working in credit risk taught me that behind every model, score and threshold is a decision — who gets approved, what gets flagged, where risk is taken, and what happens when the system gets it wrong.

That perspective now shapes how I build machine-learning and AI systems: not just whether the model works, but whether the system around it can be evaluated, explained, monitored and trusted.

My work spans credit risk, fraud detection and agentic AI, with a growing focus on AI engineering and evaluation.

“I'm interested in the space between ‘the model works’ and ‘this system is ready to be trusted.’”

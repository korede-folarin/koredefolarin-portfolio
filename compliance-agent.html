Banking Compliance Agent.

An agentic AI system for reviewing lending documents against financial regulation, surfacing evidence, uncertainty and cases requiring human review.

01

Problem

Compliance review is slow, manual and evidence-heavy.

Loan and policy documents must be checked against a large regulatory corpus. The challenge is not simply generating an answer. It is retrieving the right evidence, exposing uncertainty and making escalation explicit.

02

Approach

RAG first, tools second, human judgement preserved.

The system combines retrieval over regulatory material with bounded tool access, structured compliance reasoning, a deterministic confidence gate and an auditable report.

03

Key decision

Designed for trade-offs, not demos.

RAG supports traceable evidence and easier updates. A focused orchestration flow avoids unnecessary multi-agent complexity, while tool access remains deliberately bounded.

04

Evaluation

Fluent answers are not enough.

Retrieval quality, grounding, abstention behaviour, latency and deterministic checks are treated as first-class evaluation targets.

05

Reliability

Trust is part of the architecture.

Human review is triggered by uncertainty. Outputs preserve source traceability and an audit trail so confidence alone is never treated as evidence.

06

Reflection

The hardest failure was silent, not dramatic.

A pipeline can complete successfully while producing poor content. That led to stronger validation of ingestion quality, not just pipeline completion.

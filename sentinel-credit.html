Sentinel Credit.

A BNPL risk intelligence platform for thin-file customers, combining modelling, monitoring, explainability and governance around credit decisions.

View GitHub ↗All projects →
01

Problem

Thin-file customers create a difficult credit decisioning problem.

Traditional credit history can be limited, so the system must make careful use of behavioural, affordability and repayment signals without confusing missing history with low risk.

02

Approach

Treat risk as a system, not a single score.

The architecture brings together feature engineering, model comparison, calibration, explainability, monitoring and decision controls.

03

Validation

Temporal thinking matters.

Validation is designed to reflect how the model will meet future applicants, with attention to leakage, drift and changing population behaviour.

04

Explainability

Risk decisions need evidence.

Model outputs are paired with explanations that help a reviewer understand what drove a result rather than presenting a score in isolation.

05

Monitoring

A strong model can still become stale.

The platform treats performance, drift and changing data quality as ongoing system concerns instead of post-deployment afterthoughts.

06

Governance

Automation does not remove accountability.

Decision thresholds, human review and traceable model evidence are part of the design from the beginning.
